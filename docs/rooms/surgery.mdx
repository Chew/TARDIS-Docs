# Surgery Room

The surgery room contains a chest that:

- automatically dispenses the healing potions contained within it to players inside the TARDIS when their health drops below two hearts ❤️❤️.
- automatically dispenses milk buckets to cure players that have negative health [effects](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Effect).

<TabbedImage name={"Surgery"} description={""} folder={"rooms"} image={"surgery.jpg"} />

## Surgery chest

To use the surgery chest:

- Place healing potions and milk buckets into the chest.
- Healing and curing will happen at regular intervals (100 ticks or ~5 seconds) while players have low health and negative effects.
- Empty buckets are returned to the chest when milk is used to remove negative effects.

:::tip[Moving or adding the surgery chest]

If you want to move the surgery chest to a new location, or you have grown a surgery before the healing feature was added, you can use the update command:

`/tardis update surgery`

:::

## Configuration

In order for surgery feeding to work the config option `allow.hunger_and_healing` must be `true` in _config.yml_.
