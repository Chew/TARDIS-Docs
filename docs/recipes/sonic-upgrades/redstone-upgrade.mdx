Redstone Upgrade
===================

The Redstone Sonic Screwdriver Upgrade is useful for building redstone contraptions. It will power on redstone by right-clicking, power pistons, lamps, and redstone tracks.

## Crafting

`/trecipe redstone-upgrade`
