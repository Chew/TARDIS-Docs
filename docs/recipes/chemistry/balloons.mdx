Balloons
===================

Balloons are decorative items that you can hold. 

## Formula

`/tchemistry formula [Colour]_Balloon`

Where `[Colour]` is one of `White`, `Orange`, `Magenta`, `Light-Blue`, `Yellow`, `Lime`, `Pink`, `Gray`, `Light-Gray`, `Cyan`, `Purple`, `Blue`, `Brown`, `Green`,`Red`,  or `Black`.
